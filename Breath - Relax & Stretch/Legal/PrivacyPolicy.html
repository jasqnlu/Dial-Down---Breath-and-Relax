Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Breath - Relax & Stretch (the "App") handles your information. We built the App to work fully offline by default, and only share data with our backend when you opt in to features that require it.

1. Information Stored Locally

By default, your session history, routines, streaks, body-map annotations, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This local data is not transmitted anywhere unless you enable an optional sync or account feature described below.

2. Information We Collect When You Sign In

If you choose to create an account or sign in (including via Sign in with Apple or Google), the App may sync certain data to our backend, hosted on Supabase, in order to support:

  • Cross-device backup and sync of your progress and routines.
  • Optional social features, such as leaderboards and shared challenges.

This may include an account identifier, display name, workout/session summaries, streaks, and aggregate progress statistics. We do not collect more information than is reasonably necessary to provide these features.

3. Purchases

Subscription and purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive non-sensitive transaction status information (e.g., whether a subscription is active) in order to unlock Pro features.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except as necessary to operate the App (for example, our backend infrastructure provider, Supabase) or as required by law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear it yourself. If you have a synced account, you may request deletion of your account and associated backend data at any time via the in-app data export/delete tools or by contacting us (see below).

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact
