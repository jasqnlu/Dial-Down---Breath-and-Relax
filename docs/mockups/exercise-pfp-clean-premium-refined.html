Exercise PFP · Iteration on “Clean premium”

Clean premium, refined for real avatar sizes

Clean premium was the right base: quiet, no name-tag to lean on, reads as an icon rather than a poster. But its line weight was tuned for the 200–360px preview, not for the 20–40px sizes exercise avatars actually render at — a list row thumbnail, a tab glyph, a session-player chip. This iteration keeps clean premium's restraint and adds the two things it was missing.

  • Heavier, consistent stroke. 4.2px → 8.4px on the figure, joint dots scaled up to match — borrowed from what actually made “bold” hold up small, without the poster framing.
  • Figure ink off the accent hue. Silhouette moved from orange-on-cream to a deep umber-charcoal on cream, so the pose doesn't have to compete with the premium accent dot for contrast.
  • No text anywhere in the mark. The name-tag is gone entirely — if a pose can't read from silhouette alone, the fix is a clearer silhouette, not a label glued to the icon.

Where this leaves things: refined clean premium keeps the icon feeling calm in a list full of other icons, but it no longer quietly loses at 20–28px the way the original did. The accent dot is the one piece of “premium” branding left, and it survives shrinking because it's a filled shape, not a line. Bold's poster framing and name-tag are worth keeping in reserve for a detail page or share card, where legibility isn't the constraint — just not for the avatar itself.
