Body map · interaction study

Tap the head → the face zones fan out

Instead of hit-testing tiny boxes on the 3D face, a tap anywhere on the head expands into four labelled zone pins placed accurately over the features. You pick one, and its exercises open. This reuses the app's existing disambiguation rail — the pins, leader lines and colour-dots are the same components already shipping for muscle sub-heads. Every zone here is evidence-backed: eye-strain, TMJ and tension relief.

    Bilateral zones (3)

    Eyes, Temples, Jaw — carry a side. The side is read from where you tapped, so only four pins ever show, not seven.

    Midline zone (1)

    Forehead sits on the centre line (x ≈ 0) with no Left/Right — the first region to break the app's “everything is L/R” assumption.

    No new hit-boxes

    The head keeps its single existing box. All that's added is four hand-tuned anchor points and the exercises — accuracy becomes a tuning job, not 3D geometry.

    One decision this raises

    How do we choose the side for a bilateral zone? We infer it from the tapped x-position (tap the left jaw → “Left Jaw”), keeping the fan to four pins instead of showing all seven at once. Confirmed — this is the approach going into the spec.
