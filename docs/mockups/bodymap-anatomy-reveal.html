Prototype for the SceneKit rebuild (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-19-anatomy-model-replacement-design.md). Tap Mark, then tap anywhere — it resolves to the nearest region and offers only its anatomically-adjacent neighbours (a chest tap never offers the arm). Joints highlight as capsules; a face tap fades the skin head.
