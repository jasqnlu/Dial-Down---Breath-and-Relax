Pose glyph exploration

Make the pose tell the story

The glyph should show the exercise's defining action, not only the general body position. The quad-stretch sample below uses one bent leg, a reaching hand, and the held foot so it reads as an actual quad stretch at a glance.

Standing Quad Stretch

The rear foot is intentionally lifted high and connected to the reaching hand. The planted leg and balance arm make the silhouette stable instead of looking like a generic standing figure.

This is the level of specificity to aim for: a viewer should be able to name the exercise from the silhouette before reading the label.
